Our story

How we got started

It began with a simple idea: good tools should be easy to use. We wanted to build something that people enjoy every day.

Over the years the team grew, and so did our understanding of what matters. We learned to listen first and build second.

Simple things done well beat complex things done halfway.

Today we serve thousands of customers, and the idea is still the same. We keep things clear, honest and useful.

Thank you for being part of the journey. There is much more to come.
